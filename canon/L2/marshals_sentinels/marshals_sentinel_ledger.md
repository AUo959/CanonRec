### LEDGER-MISSIONS-0001 — Operation Silent Dagger (Mission Record)
**Source:** SRC-0005

**Classification:** “Classified – Eyes Only” (in-world document)

**Mission synopsis:** Covert strike on **Blackreach Station** (pirate-controlled deep-space facility) to neutralize a rogue AI research facility, eliminate leadership, and extract intelligence.

**Key actors (from SRC-0005):**
- **Commander Aric Thal** — Sentinel‑Praetor; mission author/lead.
- **Lior Serath** — Sentinel‑Phantom; infiltrator; compromised security grid.
- **Veyna Koris** — Sentinel‑Striker; breach + close-quarters eliminations.
- **Kael Voss** — Sentinel‑Stalker; comms monitoring; hacking/entry; AI core shutdown support.
- **Darek Voss** — Sentinel‑Vanguard; shockwave weapons; rear-guard; KIA.

**Assets used:**
- **Phantom-Class Stealth Frigate** — insertion platform using cloaking array.
- **Pirate gunship** in Dock C — seized/used for extraction.

**Tactics & tech observed (ties into prior lore):**
- **Cloaking array** defeated standard scans (ship-level stealth).
- **Thruster short-burst EVA** insertion; **magnetic boots** on hull.
- **Controlled breach** (charge used for seamless metal severance; no loud explosion).
- **Phantom optical cloaking** + security grid disruption; “four-minute window” before backup triggers.
- **Striker dual plasma blades** used for silent close kills.
- **Vanguard shockwave generator/gauntlet/cannon**: localized kinetic blast; crowd clearing.
- **Praetor wrist-mounted pulse accelerator** delivering a “neural-disrupting pulse.”
- **Stalker gauntlet hacking suite** interfacing with core systems; triggered “system purge.”

**Opposition profile:**
- Mercenary guards (former Union soldiers turned pirates).
- Hardened ex-military officers + rogue AI researchers.
- AI combat drones (half-built) activated under emergency override; used energy claws.

**Outcome:**
- AI research facility destroyed; leadership neutralized; intelligence extracted.
- **Casualties:** 1 KIA — Darek Voss (Sentinel‑Vanguard).
- Strategic effect: pirate presence crippled; illegal AI research shut down; Union intelligence gained access to rogue comm networks.

**Continuity flags:**
- “Precision elimination / silent kills” implies Marshals conduct lethal direct action—needs alignment with **Union law / Marshal charter**.
- “System purge” and “reactor overheat” suggests sabotage cascade; define how often this is an intended doctrine vs improvisation.
- Family link: Kael Voss + Darek Voss share surname; relationship unknown (possible siblings or kin).

### LEDGER-SENTINEL-0001 — Sentinel-Class Power Suit (Hardware)
**What it is:** The “pinnacle of battlefield adaptability and enforcement technology,” enabling Marshal-Operatives to exceed conventional infantry/security capabilities.

**Design intent:** “One-person fortress” with mobility + survivability + offensive capability.

**Known capabilities (from SRC-0001):**
1. **Adaptive energy shielding** (plasma/kinetic/EMP adaptation; localized reinforcement; energy redistribution)
2. **Self-sustaining power core** (mini fusion + quantum battery; ambient energy conversion)
3. **Weapons suite** (integrated + hardpoint compatible; see Weapon Ledger)
4. **Secure comms + counter-jamming** (triple-encrypted quantum entanglement; counter-surveillance)
5. **Limited matter replication** (nano-fab ammo/components/medical nanites; pattern-limited)
6. **Life + cognitive support** (atmosphere; neural link; emergency neural stabilization)
7. **Mobility** (strength amplification; mag-lock talons; zero-G controls)
8. **Short-range flight** (micro-thrusters; vector-shift maneuvering)
9. **Sabotage + apprehension** (EM hacking; stasis emitters; shockwave generators)

**Suit lighting systems (from SRC-0006):**
- **HUD / status indicators:** visual readouts for armor integrity, shield level, power core, and other mission-critical systems.
- **Squad identification:** patterns/colors signal unit designation, rank, or combat role.
- **Targeting assistance:** lighting shifts indicate active weapon systems, aiming trajectories, sensor-lock status.
- **Neural sync confirmation:** lighting can signal full neural integration; flicker/dimming can indicate interference or cyberwarfare.
- **AI co-processing feedback:** diagnostics for embedded combat AI status (if present).
- **Stealth vs presence modes:** stealth models (Phantom) can dim/shift to reduce detectability; heavy/command models (Praetor) may emphasize intimidation.
- **Hazard detection:** lighting shifts warn of radiation, toxic atmospheres, or electronic disruption.
- **Psychological warfare:** glow is an intentional intimidation/morale tool, reinforcing Sentinel symbolism.

**Security controls (from SRC-0001):**
- Suit is **biometrically linked** to the assigned trainee/operator to prevent unauthorized use.

**Program constraints (from SRC-0002 & SRC-0004):**
- Unit cost is **immense**, compared to **small starships per unit**.
- Manufacturing/deployment are **strictly limited** by the Galactic Union.
- **Maintenance load:** power cores require regular maintenance; damage repairs can take **weeks**.
- **Specialized infrastructure:** fuel/ammunition/shield regeneration require dedicated support.
- Losing an operator is treated as losing **an entire squad** worth of conventional capability.

**Unknowns / To confirm:**
- Suit mass, size class, armor materials, thermal signature management.
- Limitations (heat soak, EMP edge-cases, quantum comm range constraints).
- Maintenance + logistics chain; nano-fab feedstock.
- Shield regeneration requirements and what counts as “fuel” in practice.
- Lighting taxonomy by variant (Praetor vs Phantom vs Warden etc.) and what “stealth dim” means against various sensor types.

### LEDGER-WEAPONS-0001 — Sentinel Primary Weapon Systems (Weapons)
**Source:** SRC-0007

**Design philosophy:** Weapons must balance power, energy draw, weight, and mission utility within Union tech constraints.

**Primary systems:**

1. **Arc‑Pulse Rifle** *(standard issue)*
   - Medium-range directed-energy weapon; ionizing arc pulses for near-instant strikes.
   - Linked to suit power core via rechargeable plasma capacitors.
   - Modes: lethal/non-lethal; HUD smart-targeting; emergency overcharge → short-range EMP burst.

2. **Grav‑Flux Repeater** *(Striker / Vanguard)*
   - Kinetic/energy hybrid firing graviton-infused projectiles; impact increases with distance.
   - Micro-mass accelerators + gravimetric cores.
   - Modes: single/burst/suppression; armor-piercing or anti-shield configs.
   - Requires exo-frame stabilization due to recoil.

3. **Null‑Lance** *(Phantom / Stalker)*
   - Precision anti-materiel “rail lance”: supercooled magnetic accelerator launches ultra-dense projectiles.
   - Superconducting rails with independent charge coils.
   - Stealth features: silent firing; no visible energy signature; collapsible barrel.
   - Micro-adjusting grip for real-time targeting recalibration.

4. **Aegis Gauntlets** *(Warden / Vanguard)*
   - Reinforced power gauntlets with shockwave dispersal on impact.
   - Direct exoskeleton servo integration.
   - Effects: breach reinforced materials; directional kinetic shockwave knockback.
   - Energy redistribution can recharge suit shielding on impact.

5. **Vortex Plasma Carbine** *(Phantom / Striker)*
   - Variable energy disruptor with rotating dispersion coil.
   - Adjustable spread/intensity/range.
   - Modes: focused beam (precision) vs wide burst (close combat).
   - Integrated hacking subroutines to disrupt AI-operated shielding.
   - Lightweight for high-mobility operations.

6. **Singularity Warhead Launcher** *(Vanguard / Warden)*
   - Heavy launcher firing gravity-dense micro-warheads; briefly collapses localized gravitational fields.
   - Powered by mini singularity containment core.
   - Limited-use; self-regenerating ammo via micro-matter synthesis.
   - Requires power stabilization implants.
   - Alternate mode: repulsion → defensive gravity fields.

**Modular attachments:**
- Anti-AI override module (hacking-based disruption rounds)
- Electromagnetic disruptor coil (anti-drone)
- High-density plasma capacitors (overclocked output)
- Tactical hardlight bayonet
- Smart-link targeting HUD (long-range shot prediction)

**Continuity flags:**
- Singularity micro-warheads + gravity-field effects are high-impact tech; define strict limits (ammo cap, cooldown, collateral risk).
- “No visible energy signature” for Null‑Lance: clarify whether it’s *optical only* (still thermal/EM detectable) to keep stealth grounded.

### LEDGER-SENTINEL-0003 — Sentinel Variants (Hardware / Doctrine)
**What it is:** Mission-specialized Sentinel models, retaining core suit capabilities but optimized for distinct roles.

**Variant list and known details (from SRC-0002):**

1. **Sentinel‑Praetor** — *Command & Tactical Coordination*
   - **Role:** Battlefield command / “Field Marshal” unit.
   - **Active service:** **~30**.
   - **Features:** enhanced command uplink; fleet‑level comms across planetary engagements; personal shield enhancement via energy redistribution; integrated electronic warfare (hacking mid‑combat).

2. **Sentinel‑Striker** — *Rapid Assault / Shock Trooper*
   - **Role:** CQC and ship boarding.
   - **Active service:** **~250**.
   - **Features:** dual overcharged plasma blades; enhanced zero‑G thrusters; kinetic dampening for breach survivability; hardlight riot suppression for non‑lethal work.

3. **Sentinel‑Warden** — *Defense & Crowd Control*
   - **Role:** Urban security and riot suppression.
   - **Active service:** **~400**.
   - **Features:** mobile hardlight barriers; stun disruptors; heavy armor plating for sustained fire; AI‑controlled drone swarm support.

4. **Sentinel‑Vanguard** — *Heavy Assault & Siege*
   - **Role:** Heavy infantry / high‑risk deployments.
   - **Active service:** **<150**.
   - **Features:** siege‑class shoulder shockwave cannons; enhanced gravitic armor (short‑duration capital‑grade fire tolerance); strength enhancement to break fortifications; **mini jump drive** for short‑range teleportation.
   - **Field observation (SRC-0005):** Vanguard systems include shockwave generator/gauntlet/cannon; can hold a corridor against squads while absorbing sustained plasma fire until shields fail.

5. **Sentinel‑Phantom** — *Stealth & Covert Operations*
   - **Role:** Infiltration / assassination / sabotage.
   - **Active service:** **Unknown (highly classified)**.
   - **Features:** optical cloaking (short duration); neural dampeners resisting psionic/biometric scans; silent zero‑G thrusters; cyberwarfare for planetary defense hacking.
   - **Field observation (SRC-0005):** Phantom role includes compromising security grids and creating “time windows” before backup protocols trigger.

6. **Sentinel‑Stalker** — *Hunter‑Killer*
   - **Role:** Tracking high‑value targets and rogue agents.
   - **Active service:** **<50**.
   - **Features:** biometric targeting lock across planetary systems; long‑range cloaking for urban/wilderness; neural‑pulse disruptors (anti‑cybernetic); high‑frequency enhanced blades (anti‑armor).
   - **Field observation (SRC-0005):** Stalker includes gauntlet hacking suite and comms monitoring; can drive system purge/AI core shutdown operations.

**Updated scale notes (from SRC-0004):**
- **Total active Sentinel operatives:** **~850–1,000**.
- Sentinel operators represent **~5–10% of total Marshal forces**.
- **Single-battle constraint:** typically **<100** deployed unless emergency; extreme scenarios can reach **500+**.

**Continuity flags:**
- “Mini jump drive teleportation” remains a major power escalation; define range/cooldown/counterplay.
- Per-variant counts sum to **~830 + Phantom unknown**; consistent with the **~850–1,000** band if Phantom is modest-to-medium in number.
- “Urban incident” doctrine includes an “assassinate HVT” phrasing in SRC-0004; reconcile with Union law / Marshal charter (are Marshals allowed to conduct extrajudicial elimination?).

## 4) Extrapolated Canon Requirements (Working Theory)
These entries are *not* primary sources; they are what “should exist” given constraints already established (scarcity, maintenance bottlenecks, political limits, and bounded tech).

### LEDGER-EXTRAP-0001 — Tech Envelope & Hard Limits (Physics / Cost)
**Status:** Working theory

- **Heat is the real limiter:** fusion power may be “near-indefinite,” but sustained shielding/flight/weapons should cause **thermal saturation** → forced cooldown/venting/retreat.
- **Matter replication requires feedstock:** nano-fab should consume standardized **mass cartridges** + restricted **pattern libraries** (prevents plot-hole printing).
- **Quantum comms are low-bandwidth secure handshakes:** treat “entangled comms” as **key exchange / authentication**, not unlimited data pipes; bulk data uses classical channels (jam/spoof possible).
- **Cloaking is spectrum-limited:** strong against standard scans, but vulnerable to **thermal**, **gravitic**, particulate/dust optics, and active counter-detection grids.

### LEDGER-EXTRAP-0002 — Marshal Charter & Rules of Engagement (Law / Governance)
**Status:** Working theory

What should exist to prevent “space cops with unlimited kill authority” drift:
- **Marshal Charter Articles:** jurisdiction, cross-border authority, warrants, oversight, reporting.
- **Escalation ladder:** non-lethal default → lethal thresholds → existential-threat clauses.
- **Black-ops authorization track:** formal order types for covert missions (signatories, audit trail, veto authority).
- **After-action truth discipline:** mandatory sensor logs + independent review to prevent institutional drift.

### LEDGER-EXTRAP-0003 — Readiness & Maintenance Model (Logistics)
**Status:** Working theory

Given “repairs take weeks” + ~850–1,000 operators:
- **Readiness tiers (R1–R5):**
  - R1 mission-ready now; R2 ready in 72h; R3 routine maintenance; R4 refit/repair; R5 training/medical hold.
- **Sortie budget:** max-output minutes before mandatory service.
- **Depot infrastructure:** shield regen rigs, reactor servicing, neural-link calibration labs.
- **Supply-chain dependence:** a single contractor bottleneck (e.g., Armada Nova Systems) creates political leverage and sabotage risk.

### LEDGER-EXTRAP-0004 — Suit ↔ Ship Doctrine & Taxonomy (Doctrine)
**Status:** Working theory

- **Taxonomy lock:** “Sentinel Suit Program” vs “Sentinel Fleet Designation” to prevent naming collision confusion.
- **Carrier doctrine for Sentinel-Class Tactical Carriers:** suit bays + rapid drop capability; comms authentication node; drone overwatch coordination; embedded legal/oversight package.

### LEDGER-EXTRAP-0005 — Big-Tech Boundaries (Balance)
**Status:** Working theory

**Vanguard mini jump drive (teleport):**
- Very short range; line-of-sight or beacon requirement; cooldown + heat spike; failure modes; counterplay via mass-lock/jump-scramblers.

**Singularity warhead launcher:**
- Extremely limited ammo; high collateral risk; rare authorization; severe political consequences.

**Null-Lance “no visible signature”:**
- Optical-quiet only; still thermal/EM residue detectable by high-grade sensors.
