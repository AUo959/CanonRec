# GUMAS L2 GALACTIC MAP — SOURCE OF TRUTH (v1.0)

**Purpose**
This document is the canonical, load-bearing reference for the physical layout, power distribution, and structural constraints of the L2 Galactic Union Multi‑Agent Simulation (GUMAS). All future discoveries, narratives, factions, cultures, and events must be consistent with this map. Where conflict arises, the map takes precedence.

---

## 1. Epistemic Rule
**Detail is discovered, not invented.**
Narrative records what the universe reveals under constraint. Physical placement precedes culture, ideology, and character.

## 2. Macro Galactic Structure

### 2.1 Inner Mid‑Disk Core Zone
**Traits:**
- Highest stellar density
- Stable, mature star populations
- Dense jump‑gate / hyperlane mesh

**Occupants:**
- Galactic Union Administrative Core
- Union Industrial / Fleet Hub
- Union Economic Capital (Vel‑Surak) *(placed by owner ruling 2026‑07‑21)*
- Prime Construct Primary Enclave (embedded)

**Function:** Bureaucratic and logistical gravity well. Enables Union coherence but creates institutional inertia.

---

### 2.2 Union Gradient Frontier
**Traits:**
- Declining gate density
- Increasing transit lag
- Mixed hazard pockets

**Occupants:**
- Union Forward Bastions
- Union‑aligned Velar worlds
- Sovereign Front / Autonomist clusters
- Zyphari secondary ports
- Prime Construct transit‑adjacent nodes

**Function:** Zone where Union authority degrades gradually. Political ambiguity is enforced by physics.

---

### 2.3 Velar Crescent (Post‑Imperial Fault Line)
**Traits:**
- Uneven connectivity
- Legacy infrastructure + decay
- Heavy historical scars

**Occupants:**
- Velar Core World (old Imperium capital)
- Velar Border Worlds
- Velar Ruin Worlds
- Velar Outer Colony Nodes

**Function:** Explains permanent Velar fragmentation, divide‑and‑rule politics, and endemic instability.

---

### 2.4 Contested Frontier Band
**Traits:**
- Secondary corridors only
- High hazard density
- No durable enforcement

**Occupants:**
- Separatist Confederation chokepoints
- Zyphari high‑risk trade nodes
- Pirate kingdoms / warlord systems
- Access routes to Dyson Frontier

**Function:** Entropy sink. Power is leverage‑based, not territorial.

---

### 2.5 Dyson Sphere Frontier
**Traits:**
- Two abandoned Dyson spheres (twin systems)
- Lethal approach conditions
- Derelict‑choked orbital volumes

**Occupants:**
- No sovereign state
- Scavenger fleets
- Pirate confederations

**Function:** Forbidden prize. Explains why no hegemon dominates the galaxy.

---

### 2.6 Nebular Hive Complex
**Traits:**
- Filamentary nebulae
- Active stellar nurseries
- Severe survey degradation

**Occupants:**
- Hidden habitable systems
- Multiple pre‑contact civilizations
- Occasional local spacefaring societies

**Function:** Latent diversity reservoir. Keeps discovery alive.

---

### 2.7 The Great Interstice
**Traits:**
- Vast low‑density stellar desert
- Infrastructure non‑viable
- Navigation inefficient but possible

**Occupants:**
- Almost none

**Function:** Negative structure. Delays contact histories and prevents full galactic unification.

## 3. Background Astrophysical Distribution

- **Pulsars:** Concentrated in older, dense regions (Inner Mid‑Disk, spiral remnants)
- **Magnetars:** Rare, clustered near recent massive star formation; hard no‑go bubbles
- **Quasar‑like activity:** Central remnant only; shapes inner radiation geometry
- **Supernova remnants:** Clustered along spiral arms and nebular outskirts
- **Stellar‑mass black holes:** Unevenly scattered, higher density in old regions

These phenomena are terrain, not landmarks.

### 8.2 GU-LOG-01 — Union Industrial & Fleet Hub System

**Status:** 🔓 Placed (Historically Layered)

**Designation:** GU-LOG-01 (provisional system ID)
**System Name:** *Pending ratification*

**Zone:** Inner-to-Mid Disk Transition (Union Gradient Frontier, inward edge)

**Stellar & Orbital Profile (Constrained):**
- Stable primary with resource-rich outer belt
- Multiple inhabited worlds with differentiated development paths
- Extensive orbital yards and industrial rings layered atop older infrastructure

**Historical Layering (Critical):**
- Early Expansion Era: Initially settled for raw material extraction and local manufacturing
- Consolidation Period: Became a fleet assembly and refit center during early inter-polity conflicts
- Union Formation: Industrial capacity federated rather than nationalized, preserving local power blocs
- Modern Era: Functions as the logistical spine of Union power, but retains internal political fragmentation

**Internal Planetary Diversity (Non-Monolithic):**
- Core World: Administrative-industrial hybrid world hosting coordination ministries and legacy factories
- Yard Worlds: Orbital-dominated habitats focused on ship construction and refit
- Labor Worlds: High-population planets with strong union movements and historical labor unrest
- Peripheral Habitats: Older settlements economically marginalized but culturally entrenched

**Functional Role (Present-Day):**
- Primary fleet construction and maintenance hub
- Logistics coordination for Union-wide deployments
- Emergency production surge capacity

**Political & Social Tensions (Now Enforced):**
- Persistent labor politics influencing fleet readiness
- Tension between local industrial autonomy and Union strategic demands
- Economic dependency on continued Union military relevance

**Security & Enforcement:**
- Heavy defensive presence scaled for industrial protection
- Significant counter-sabotage and internal security operations
- Not optimized for frontier combat response

**Constraints (Non-Negotiable):**
- Must remain within rapid transit range of GU-CORE-01
- Cannot monopolize all Union heavy industry
- Cannot be politically homogeneous

**Immediate Consequences:**
- Union military power is bottlenecked here
- Disruption produces cascading delays rather than instant collapse
- This system generates as many political crises as it solves

### 8.3 ZY-TRADE-01 — Zyphari Primary Trade Nexus System

**Status:** 🔓 Placed (Historically Layered)

**Designation:** ZY-TRADE-01 (provisional system ID)
**System Name:** *Pending ratification*

**Zone:** Inner Mid-Disk Core Zone (Adjacency to Union Core)

**Stellar & Orbital Profile (Constrained):**
- Stable primary with unusually favorable transit geometry
- Multiple inner-system habitable worlds alongside dense orbital habitats
- Extensive artificial transit infrastructure predating modern Union gates

**Historical Layering (Non-Monolithic):**
- Pre-Union Era: Emerged as a neutral exchange system at a natural convergence of early hyperlane routes
- Early Corporate Era: Zyphari interests consolidated port authority through contracts, debt instruments, and infrastructure stewardship rather than conquest
- Union Formation: System formally recognized as a neutral commercial hub under Union law, preserving Zyphari operational autonomy
- Modern Era: Serves as the economic fulcrum of inter-polity trade, finance, and logistics

**Internal Planetary & Orbital Diversity:**
- Port World: Planet-scale spaceport with layered jurisdictions, customs regimes, and arbitration courts
- Finance Habitats: Orbital arcologies hosting banks, clearinghouses, and data exchanges
- Legacy Settlements: Older populations displaced from direct port control but embedded in service economies
- Industrial Shadows: Peripheral manufacturing and ship services tied to trade flows

**Functional Role (Present-Day):**
- Primary galactic trade clearing nexus
- Contract arbitration and standards enforcement (commercial, not political)
- Logistical coordination across Union, Velar, and frontier markets

**Political & Economic Tensions (Now Enforced):**
- Zyphari leverage over Union logistics without territorial sovereignty
- Persistent jurisdictional conflict between Union regulators and corporate authorities
- Economic dependency of neighboring systems on trade throughput

**Security & Enforcement:**
- Privately contracted security fleets operating under layered legal mandates
- Heavy surveillance optimized for fraud, sabotage, and market manipulation
- Reluctant reliance on Union Marshalls only in extreme cases

**Constraints (Non-Negotiable):**
- Must remain economically indispensable to multiple powers
- Cannot be fully sovereign or fully regulated
- Cannot be culturally homogeneous

**Immediate Consequences:**
- Union policy is indirectly shaped here through economic pressure
- Disruption produces market shockwaves rather than military crises
- Conflicts manifest as contract wars, embargoes, and data sabotage

### 8.4 GU-FRONT-01 — Union Frontier Bastion System

**Status:** 🔓 Placed (Historically Layered)

**Designation:** GU-FRONT-01 (provisional system ID)
**System Name:** *Pending ratification*

**Zone:** Outer Edge of Union Gradient Frontier (Approach to Contested Space)

**Stellar & Orbital Profile (Constrained):**
- Stable but older primary star
- Fewer naturally habitable worlds; heavy reliance on orbital habitats and terraforming
- Extensive legacy defense platforms intermixed with newer Union installations

**Historical Layering (Non-Monolithic):**
- Early Expansion Era: Initially settled as a mixed civilian–military waypoint guarding a high-value corridor
- Imperial Border Period: Fortified repeatedly during Velar expansion and counter-expansion cycles
- Union Consolidation: Absorbed into Union jurisdiction after prolonged negotiations with local authorities
- Modern Era: Serves as a symbolic and practical edge of Union order

**Internal System Diversity:**
- Bastion World: Heavily defended planet hosting Union Marshalls command and rapid-response coordination
- Civilian Trade World: Commercial hub serving frontier traffic, refugees, and contractors
- Veteran Habitats: Orbital settlements dominated by retired personnel and multigenerational service families
- Peripheral Settlements: Older colonies resistant to central oversight

**Functional Role (Present-Day):**
- Forward deployment and coordination of Union Marshalls
- Staging ground for diplomacy, peacekeeping, and limited military intervention
- Intelligence relay point monitoring Velar, Separatist, and pirate movements

**Political & Social Tensions (Now Enforced):**
- Ongoing tension between security mandates and civilian autonomy
- Reformist pressure to demilitarize versus hardline calls for fortification
- Cultural divide between core-born administrators and frontier populations

**Security & Enforcement:**
- High readiness defensive posture
- Rapid-response forces optimized for containment, not conquest
- Persistent strain on personnel and infrastructure

**Constraints (Non-Negotiable):**
- Must remain within extended but delayed transit range of GU-LOG-01
- Cannot permanently pacify surrounding frontier space
- Cannot function without local cooperation

**Immediate Consequences:**
- Union authority beyond this point becomes conditional
- Crises here escalate quickly but resolve slowly
- This system defines the lived experience of "the Union" for frontier populations

### 8.5 VEL-BORDER-01 — Velar Border World System

**Status:** 🔓 Placed (Historically Layered)

**Designation:** VEL-BORDER-01 (provisional system ID)
**System Name:** *Pending ratification*

**Zone:** Velar Crescent — Direct Adjacency to GU-FRONT-01

**Stellar & Orbital Profile (Constrained):**
- Moderately active primary star
- One primary habitable world with multiple moons
- Secondary bodies adapted via Velari physiological and technological means

**Historical Layering (Non-Monolithic):**
- Pre-Imperial Era: Independently settled Velari world with mixed clan and civic governance
- Imperial Expansion: Absorbed into the Velar Imperium as a strategic border system; heavy militarization followed
- Post-Fracture Period: Control fragmented among local elites, military governors, and off-world interests
- Modern Era: Exists in a state of suspended legitimacy, claimed by multiple Velar factions

**Internal Planetary Diversity:**
- Capital Arcology: Once an imperial administrative center, now divided among competing authorities
- Militarized Zones: Legacy fortifications repurposed by rival Velar forces
- Civilian Regions: Populations adapted to prolonged instability, informal economies dominate
- Peripheral Settlements: Marginalized communities exploited by smuggling and mercenary networks

**Functional Role (Present-Day):**
- Buffer and flashpoint between Union and Velar influence
- Recruitment ground for Velar loyalists, reformists, and warlords alike
- Intelligence battleground with overlapping covert operations

**Political & Social Tensions (Now Enforced):**
- Chronic legitimacy crisis: no authority fully accepted
- Civilian distrust of all external powers
- Constant pressure from Union diplomacy and Velar internal rivalries

**Security & Enforcement:**
- Patchwork military presence with shifting allegiances
- Local defense forces better at survival than territorial control
- High civilian exposure to violence and coercion

**Constraints (Non-Negotiable):**
- Cannot be politically stable
- Cannot fully align with the Union without triggering Velar retaliation
- Cannot be ignored by GU-FRONT-01

**Immediate Consequences:**
- Any crisis here risks Union–Velar escalation
- Acts as a mirror reflecting Velar fragmentation
- Shapes frontier narratives on both sides of the border

### 8.6 VEL-CORE-01 — Velar Core World System

**Status:** 🔓 Placed (Historically Layered)

**Designation:** VEL-CORE-01 (provisional system ID)
**System Name:** *Pending ratification*

**Zone:** Inner Velar Crescent (Former Imperial Heartland)

**Stellar & Orbital Profile (Constrained):**
- Stable, luminous primary star long favored for settlement
- Multiple inner habitable worlds with dense population layers
- Extensive orbital infrastructure dating back to peak Imperial expansion

**Historical Layering (Non-Monolithic):**
- Pre-Imperial Era: Independent Velari polities competing across several worlds in-system
- Imperial Ascendancy: System unified through conquest, treaty, and dynastic consolidation; became symbolic and administrative heart of the Imperium
- Late Imperial Period: Increasing bureaucratic centralization and elite insulation from frontier realities
- Post-Fracture Era: Retains symbolic authority but fractured governance; power distributed among rival institutions claiming continuity

**Internal System Diversity:**
- Throne World: Former seat of Imperial authority; now a contested center of ritual legitimacy, archives, and ceremonial power
- Administrative Worlds: Bureaucratic planets hosting legacy ministries, courts, and academies
- Elite Habitats: Orbital and surface enclaves for aristocratic houses and senior officers
- Legacy Industrial Zones: Aging infrastructure supporting fleets and prestige projects

**Functional Role (Present-Day):**
- Cultural and symbolic anchor of Velar identity
- Source of ideological narratives used by Loyalists, Reformists, and rivals alike
- Training and credentialing center for Velar elites

**Political & Social Tensions (Now Enforced):**
- Deep disconnect between core elites and frontier populations
- Competing claims of legitimacy by multiple Velar factions
- Institutional inertia resistant to reform yet incapable of full restoration

**Security & Enforcement:**
- Heavy internal security presence focused on regime survival
- Less effective external force projection than reputation suggests
- Reliance on loyalist fleets and intelligence networks

**Constraints (Non-Negotiable):**
- Cannot exercise uncontested authority over Velar border or outer worlds
- Cannot fully disengage from Union diplomacy
- Cannot reform rapidly without destabilizing internal power balances

**Immediate Consequences:**
- Velar politics orbit this system even when power lies elsewhere
- Decisions here echo symbolically across the Velar Crescent
- Decline is managed, not resolved
