## Ranger Field-Drone Complement

**Canon ruling — 2026-07-21:** Ranger crews routinely carry a mission-configurable family of deployable drones. Drone use is an ordinary extension of Ranger fieldcraft, not a specialist capability reserved for major operations.

Typical Ranger drone functions include:

- **Situational-awareness drones:** compact aerial, surface, and interior scouts used for route inspection, perimeter awareness, multispectrum observation, pursuit tracking, and persistent overwatch.
- **Mapping and relay drones:** extend communications and sensor coverage, map unfamiliar structures or terrain, and maintain links through obstructed or degraded environments.
- **Evidence and recovery drones:** inspect hazardous scenes, mark or recover material, document conditions, and preserve evidence-chain records.
- **Porter and resupply drones:** deliver ammunition, medical supplies, power cells, tools, replacement components, and mission-specific equipment from the Ranger gunboat or a remote carrier.
- **Decoy and countermeasure drones:** create false signatures, expose hostile sensors, assist withdrawal, and contest enemy unmanned systems.
- **FPV precision munitions:** expendable, operator-directed drones used against validated hostile personnel, vehicles, weapons, or infrastructure when authorized under normal Marshal use-of-force rules. They do not possess independent lethal-engagement authority.

### Employment Doctrine

- Rangers deploy drones naturally during arrival, dismount, ingress, pursuit, perimeter establishment, and contact. In practiced crews, launch and tasking are often performed with little discussion unless unusual authority or risk is involved.
- The logistics officer normally manages the overall drone picture, allocation, recovery, maintenance, evidence retention, and network integrity. Field agents may directly control individual drones when tactical immediacy requires it.
- Drone feeds are fused into the crew's shared situational picture and the Ranger gunboat's human–AI cooperative systems. Drones extend perception and reach; they do not replace Ranger judgment, legal authority, or responsibility.
- Drone complements vary by mission, crew preference, available storage, and support access. No universal fixed count is established.
- Ranger gunboats serve as launch, recovery, recharge, maintenance, data-ingestion, and control platforms for their organic drone complement.
- FPV munitions require affirmative human authorization for employment and remain subject to the same necessity, proportionality, identification, and after-action evidence requirements as other Marshal weapons.

### Operational Character

Experienced Rangers treat drone deployment as habitual field preparation—closer to checking a sightline or opening a communications channel than requesting extraordinary support. A Ranger crew entering an uncertain environment without first extending some form of unmanned awareness would be considered unusually exposed unless stealth, local law, interference, or mission constraints made deployment inappropriate.
