# Dr. Amira Sato — Chief Ethics Officer

**Division:** Command & Ethics

**Clearance:** L5_ETHICS  
## Summary

Sato treats ethics as an operational system: constraints, audits, and decision logs that hold up when things go sideways. She chairs reviews, designs escalation pathways, and ensures 'we meant well' never becomes a substitute for accountable process.
